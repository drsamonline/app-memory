Memory

Keep the day, privately. Every capture is encrypted with a key only you hold, and the export is the only way anything leaves.

Open Memory
Status Built and working offline: AES-256-GCM over every capture, with the key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 150,000 rounds) and never stored; search that decrypts in memory at the moment you ask; a clip with no words kept and marked “no words heard”; and an export that is ciphertext only.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: always-listening audio capture and on-device transcription. There is no speech model in this estate, so you type the words or keep a clip marked “no words heard” — said here rather than shipping a microphone that records nothing useful.

The privacy is architectural, not a promise

  • Encrypted before it is written. The word you search for is not in the stored bytes at all — the test searches them for it and finds nothing.
  • The key is never stored. It is derived from your passphrase each time, and the passphrase is the only way in.
  • No network code anywhere. No fetch, no endpoint, no URL in the engine; CSP connect-src 'none'.
  • The export is the only way out, and it leaves encrypted.

What it will not do

  • Upload anything. There is nothing in the app that could.
  • Store a word in the clear. Not the words, not the place you typed.
  • Drop a silent clip. It is kept and marked, so a gap in the day is visible.
  • Start fresh over an unreadable store. A broken store would look like a quiet day.
  • Keep capturing quietly when storage fails. Capture stops and says so, loudly.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA capture with neither words nor a clip is refused. A passphrase under eight characters is refused.
No networkNothing changes; nothing ever leaves.
Storage full or unavailableCAPTURE STOPPED, loudly, and the oldest unsaved hour is reported lost.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with a fresh store.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable done-means is asserted there, including that the word is absent from the stored bytes.
