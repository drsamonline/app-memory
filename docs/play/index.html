Memory

Keep the day, privately. Encrypted at rest with a key only you hold.

about

The key

Your passphrase derives the key that encrypts every capture. The key is never stored, and there is no way to recover the store without it. Eight characters minimum.

Capture something

The words are encrypted before they are written, so searching the stored bytes for what you typed finds nothing.

Find it again

A word spoken once outranks a word spoken in every capture, so the one you are looking for comes first — not the most recent.

Search decrypts in memory, just now. Nothing is indexed on disk, and the index does not exist between searches.
